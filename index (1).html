CAMPUS GUIDELINES

Accepted Materials on Campus

General preparation reminders for the three categories this model recognizes.

▤

Paper

PAPER
Common examples
  • Clean sheets and notebook paper
  • Newspapers and paper flyers
  • Uncoated paper mail
Keep out
  • Greasy or food-soiled paper
  • Waxed or heavily coated paper
Tip: Keep paper dry and follow the bin label.
♻

Plastic

PLASTIC
Common examples
  • Accepted plastic bottles
  • Clean, empty rigid containers
  • Plastic packaging marked accepted locally
Keep out
  • Food or liquid residue
  • Plastic bags unless specifically accepted
Tip: Empty containers; check local rules for lids.
▧

Cardboard

CARDBOARD
Common examples
  • Clean corrugated boxes
  • Flattened shipping cartons
  • Dry, unsoiled cardboard
Keep out
  • Greasy pizza-box sections
  • Waxed or food-soiled cardboard
Tip: Flatten boxes if the local bin instructions request it.
These are general tips, not an official PCTVS waste policy. Always check current campus signage and your municipality's accepted-material list.
