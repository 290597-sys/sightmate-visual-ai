Making the world around you easier to understand.

SightMate uses an image classification model to recognize visual categories in a camera view or an uploaded photo. Results are presented in a clear, accessible format with optional spoken output.

!

Safety Notice: SightMate provides image-based predictions only. It does not verify distances, detect every obstacle, or replace a white cane, guide dog, orientation and mobility training, or help from a trusted person. Never use an AI prediction to decide when to cross a road.

How SightMate Works

3 Steps
1

Capture or Upload

Start your camera or choose an image file from your device. You control when a still image is analyzed.

2

AI Classification

The connected Teachable Machine image model returns its own class labels and confidence scores. SightMate does not invent labels.

3

Listen or Read

Review the prediction, confidence breakdown, and uncertainty notice. Use the read-aloud control if it is supported by your browser.

Core Features

◉

Image recognition
